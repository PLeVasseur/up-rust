= Which requirements an implementation follows
:toc: preamble

NOTE: This page says which requirements of this repository an implementation follows.
For some behaviours the repository holds two rules: one in a protocol chapter, and one in an item filed under `safety/`.

== Where the rules are stated, and who follows them

[cols="2,4,4",options="header"]
|===
| Rules | Where they are stated | Who follows them

| The protocol chapters
| The chapters xref:basics/README.adoc[`basics/`], xref:up-l1/README.adoc[`up-l1/`], xref:up-l2/README.adoc[`up-l2/`] and xref:up-l3/README.adoc[`up-l3/`], with the items they tag for tracing: `LanguageLibrary`, `TransportLayerImpl` and the other tags that xref:README.adoc[the repository's README] lists
| The implementations that each chapter's rules cover, as the chapter states; how a chapter's rule and an item of the safety area meet: xref:safety/README.adoc#req-ssr-contract-transition[What a part declares and claims, and when a replacement takes effect]

| The items of the safety area
| The items filed under xref:safety/README.adoc[`safety/`], which carry the `Safety…` tags
| xref:safety/README.adoc#req-ssr-requirements-bind[A requirement binds the actors to which it gives a duty]; xref:safety/seooc-up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc#aou-seooc-assumptions-bind[An assumption of use binds the integrator who claims the scope, and replaces no requirement]

| The owned-frame SDK contract
| The items filed under xref:sdk/owned-frame/README.adoc[`sdk/owned-frame/`], which carry the `OwnedFrame…` tags
| xref:sdk/owned-frame/README.adoc#req-sdk-owned-frame-contract[The owned-frame SDK contract binds the parts that declare it]
|===

[NOTE]
====
* *One implementation family.*
The items of the safety area are written for the up-rust SEooC and for the parts that work with it.
* *Kinds of claim.*
They are filed under `safety/`, and a claim that rests on them can be of more than one kind (<<claims>>).
* *Relation to the chapters.*
Most items add to the protocol chapters.
Some state a chapter's rule again with more precision, and some differ from a chapter's rule on purpose.
====

[[claims]]
== Responsibility and kind of claim

NOTE: A rule answers two questions, and neither answer follows from the other or from the directory that the rule is filed in.
Whom a rule of a protocol chapter binds is stated by that chapter, and whom an item of the safety area or of the owned-frame SDK area binds is stated by the items below.

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
| Question | Possible answers

| Whose responsibility?
| The common protocol; a contract between parts, such as a wire, a binding or an interaction; the own contract of one SDK or one library; or a duty of whoever integrates and deploys.

| What kind of claim can rest on it?
| Ordinary functional behaviour; native resource behaviour; a finite timing bound; or a safety-assurance scope, with its evidence and its assumptions of use (xref:safety/README.adoc#req-ssr-contract-transition[What a part declares and claims, and when a replacement takes effect], "What a claim covers").
|===

NOTE: So an item under `safety/` can state ordinary functional behaviour of the up-rust SEooC.

These items state when an item binds, whatever its kind of claim:

* *A software requirement*: xref:safety/README.adoc#req-ssr-requirements-bind[A requirement binds the actors to which it gives a duty].
* *An assumption of use*: xref:safety/seooc-up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc#aou-seooc-assumptions-bind[An assumption of use binds the integrator who claims the scope, and replaces no requirement].
* *A requirement of the owned-frame SDK contract*: xref:sdk/owned-frame/README.adoc#req-sdk-owned-frame-contract[The owned-frame SDK contract binds the parts that declare it].

The up-rust SEooC's own account of its claims is xref:safety/seooc-up-rust/README.adoc#claims[the SEooC's claims].
